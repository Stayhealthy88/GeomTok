The bottleneck

A circle should be one idea.
To an LLM, it's 28 fragments.

When a model tokenizes cx="150.5", it sees "1" "50" "." "5" — never a point in space. That's why AI-drawn icons come out wobbly, off-grid, and broken. We fixed the substrate.

⚠ Standard text tokenizer
One circle → 28 tokens of scrambled digits. No notion of center, radius, or roundness.
✓ GeomTok geometric tokenizer
Same circle → 5 tokens carrying center, radius & shape identity. The AI sees a circle.
Measured, not claimed

Fewer tokens — honestly.

Every number here is benchmarked against a fair baseline: a domain-trained text tokenizer at the same vocabulary size, on 400 real icons. No strawman comparisons.

Lower is better. char-BPE = a SentencePiece tokenizer trained on SVG text at vocab 5,561 — the fair comparison. GeomTok-L1 = geometric primitive tokens. +Learned-BPE = merges learned on top.

The discovery
A COUNTERINTUITIVE RESULT
Compression ≠ Modelability.
The tokenizer that compresses most is not the one AI learns best from.

Training an identical model on the same real icons — swapping only the tokenizer — geometric primitive tokens (GeomTok-L1) produced the best downstream model, beating a text tokenizer by 25%. Yet aggressive learned merges compressed 1.8× more while modeling worse — they trade token count for per-token entropy. For AI that generates vectors, the right unit is a geometric primitive, not the shortest sequence.

Under the hood

From raw SVG to geometry the AI understands.

A four-stage pipeline that flattens real-world messiness and quantizes coordinates with a vocabulary-free fixed-point codec.

01 · PARSE

Flatten

Resolve transforms & group inheritance, normalize the viewBox, drop invisible defs. 100% on real icons.

02 · QUANTIZE

Fixed-point grid

A 64×64 uniform grid reused as a base-64 numeral system — scalars to 0.04px error, zero added vocabulary.

03 · TOKENIZE

Primitives

Commands, shapes & coordinates become geometric tokens — the substrate AI models best.

04 · DECODE

Grammar-safe

An FSA constrains generation to valid SVG by construction. Render-based fidelity, not guesswork.
